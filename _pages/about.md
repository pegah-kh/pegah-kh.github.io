I am a PhD student at ISIR (Sorbonne University) under the supervision of Matthieu Cord, which I started in September 2025. **I am currently looking for research internships! feel free to reach out!**

Before my PhD, I worked as a research engineer at ISIR. I previously completed an Engineering degree at École Polytechnique (Palaiseau) and Télécom Paris. During my studies, I interned at valeo.ai, where I worked on generating adversarial scenarios for autonomous driving, under the supervision of Eloi Zablocki and Alexandre Boulch. I also interned at Vita Lab (EPFL) with Alexandre Alahi and Taylor Mordan, focusing on pose estimation methods for detecting keypoints in clothing items.

My current research interests include multimodality in vision-language models (VLMs), explainability, and efficient methods to influence and adapt these models.

Outside of work, I enjoy climbing, hiking, skating, drawing cute, meaningless things just for fun, and watching fantasy series.
